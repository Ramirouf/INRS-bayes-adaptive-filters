The review combines LMS and NLMS filters. Here its convex combination (cvx-PN-LMS, its eq.~(17) and Table~4) and its
sharing schemes (Table~5) are written for two fKFs, the filters of Algorithm~2 of the draft. Both methods then run the
same two filters, and only the weights and the sharing differ. (R$n$) is an equation of the review, (D$n$) one of the
draft.

\section*{1. The component: the fKF as a variable-step NLMS}

Filter $k$ has the fixed predicted variance $v^{(k)}$ (D37):
\begin{equation}
  e_{t,k} = y_t - \bx_t\T\bw_{t-1,k}, \qquad
  \sigma_{t,k}^2 = v^{(k)}\|\bx_t\|^2, \qquad
  \bw_{t,k} = \bw_{t-1,k} + \frac{\bx_t}{\|\bx_t\|^2}\,\chi_t(e_{t,k}),
  \label{fkf}
\end{equation}
with $\chi_t$ evaluated with $\sigma_{t,k}^2$, by (D43) for Laplacian noise. Written as an NLMS update,
\begin{equation}
  \bw_{t,k} = \bw_{t-1,k} + \mu_{t,k}\,\frac{\bx_t\,e_{t,k}}{\|\bx_t\|^2}, \qquad
  \mu_{t,k} = \frac{\chi_t(e_{t,k})}{e_{t,k}} .
  \label{vss}
\end{equation}
The step $\mu_{t,k}$ changes at every step with the error and with $\sigma_{t,k}$. Since $\chi_t$ is odd and
$0 \le \chi_t' \le 1$ for log-concave noise, $\mu_{t,k} \in [0, 1]$. For Gaussian noise (D38),
$\mu_{t,k} = v^{(k)}\|\bx_t\|^2 / (v^{(k)}\|\bx_t\|^2 + v_\eta)$, the regularized NLMS. For Laplacian noise
$|\chi_t| < \sigma_{t,k}^2/b_\eta$, so an outlier moves $\bw_{t,k}$ by a bounded amount. The variance $v^{(k)}$ plays the
role of the review's step size: filter 1 is the fast one and filter 2 the slow one, $v^{(1)} > v^{(2)}$.

\section*{2. The combination: cvx-PN-LMS}

The output (R2)--(R4), with the sigmoid (R15):
\begin{gather}
  \lambda_t = \sgm(a_t) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-a_t}}, \qquad
  \hat\bw_{t-1} = \lambda_t\bw_{t-1,1} + (1 - \lambda_t)\bw_{t-1,2}, \nonumber\\
  e_t = y_t - \bx_t\T\hat\bw_{t-1} = \lambda_t e_{t,1} + (1 - \lambda_t)e_{t,2} .
  \label{output}
\end{gather}
With $\hat y_{t,k} = \bx_t\T\bw_{t-1,k}$, $\partial e_t/\partial\lambda_t = -(\hat y_{t,1} - \hat y_{t,2})$ and
$d\lambda_t/da_t = \lambda_t(1 - \lambda_t)$. A stochastic gradient step on $e_t^2$, normalized by the power of
$\hat y_{t,1} - \hat y_{t,2}$, gives (R17), Table~4:
\begin{align}
  p_t &= \gamma\,p_{t-1} + (1 - \gamma)(\hat y_{t,1} - \hat y_{t,2})^2,
  \label{power}\\
  a_{t+1} &= \Big[\,a_t + \frac{\mu_a}{p_t}\,\lambda_t(1 - \lambda_t)\,e_t\,(\hat y_{t,1} - \hat y_{t,2})\Big]_{-a^+}^{a^+},
  \label{cvx}
\end{align}
where $[\,\cdot\,]_{-a^+}^{a^+}$ clips to $[-a^+, a^+]$ ($a^+ = 4$ in the review). $\gamma$ is the review's $\eta$,
renamed because $\eta_t$ is the noise here. $p_t = 0$ only while $\hat y_{t,1} = \hat y_{t,2}$, for example while both
filters are still at $\bzero$; $a_t$ is not moved then. Nothing in \eqref{output}--\eqref{cvx} depends on the
component filters. Replacing the review's LMS filters by fKFs changes only how $\hat y_{t,k}$ and $\bw_{t,k}$ are
produced.

After both filters' step \eqref{fkf}, with this step's $\lambda_t$:

\noindent The fKF carries no state other than $\bw_{t,k}$, so sharing $\bw$ shares the whole filter. With the sKFs of
\texttt{cvx-combination-vs-bank.ipynb} each filter kept its own $v$. The schemes are written from the review only; its
references [47]--[49] were not read.

\section*{4. Against Algorithm 2 with $K = 2$}

Both methods run the two fKF steps \eqref{fkf}. They differ in two places.

\paragraph{The weight.} The combination learns $\lambda_t$ by the gradient \eqref{cvx} on the squared output error.
The bank uses the posterior $\mu_{t,j} \propto c_{t,j}\,\pdf_{t,j}(e_{t,j})$ (D56), from each filter's predictive
density at its own error.

\paragraph{The sharing.} The bank shares through its mixing (D53)--(D54), at every step and before the update. With
$K = 2$ the transitions (D52) are $p_{12} = p_{21} = q$, and the mixing for filter 2 reads
\begin{equation}
  \bar\bw_{t,2} = \ell_{t,2}\,\bw_{t-1,2} + (1 - \ell_{t,2})\,\bw_{t-1,1}, \qquad
  \ell_{t,2} = \frac{(1 - q)\,\mu_{t-1,2}}{(1 - q)\,\mu_{t-1,2} + q\,\mu_{t-1,1}},
  \label{mixing}
\end{equation}
and the same with the indices swapped for filter 1. This is the gradual transfer of Table~5 with three differences:
$\ell_{t,j}$ is set at every step by the weights and $q$, not fixed by hand; there is no threshold, since $\ell_{t,2}$
is close to 1 while the slow filter is probable and drops when the fast one takes over; and it runs in both
directions. Each filter then steps from $\bar\bw_{t,j}$, not from $\bw_{t-1,j}$.

\begin{algorithm}[H]
\caption{Convex combination of two fKF filters (cvx-PN-LMS) with weight sharing}
\label{alg:cvx.fKF}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require fixed predicted variances $v^{(1)} > v^{(2)}$ (fast, slow), step $\mu_a$, forgetting factor $\gamma$,
         bound $a^+$, noise scale $b_\eta$; a sharing scheme and its parameters ($\ell$, $\lambda_0$, $N_0$)
\State $\bw_{0,k} \gets \bzero$ for $k = 1, 2$; $a_1 \gets 0$, $\lambda_1 \gets 1/2$, $p_0 \gets 0$
\For{$t = 1, 2, \ldots$}
  \State receive $\bx_t$ and $y_t$
  \For{$k = 1, 2$}
    \Comment{one fKF step per filter}
    \State $\sigma^2 \gets v^{(k)}\|\bx_t\|^2$,\; $\hat y_k \gets \bx_t\T\bw_{t-1,k}$,\; $e_k \gets y_t - \hat y_k$
    \State $\bw_{t,k} \gets \bw_{t-1,k} + \bx_t\,\chi_t(e_k)/\|\bx_t\|^2$
  \EndFor
  \State $e \gets y_t - \lambda_t\hat y_1 - (1 - \lambda_t)\hat y_2$,\;
         $p_t \gets \gamma p_{t-1} + (1 - \gamma)(\hat y_1 - \hat y_2)^2$
         \Comment{cvx-PN-LMS, \eqref{output}--\eqref{power}}
  \State $a_{t+1} \gets a_t + \mu_a\lambda_t(1 - \lambda_t)\,e\,(\hat y_1 - \hat y_2)/p_t$ if $p_t > 0$, else $a_t$;
         clip to $[-a^+, a^+]$
         \Comment{\eqref{cvx}}
  \If{gradual transfer and $\lambda_t \ge \lambda_0$}
    \Comment{sharing, Table 5}
    \State $\bw_{t,2} \gets \ell\,\bw_{t,2} + (1 - \ell)\,\bw_{t,1}$
  \ElsIf{simple copy and $\lambda_t \ge \lambda_0$ and $t = mN_0$}
    \State $\bw_{t,2} \gets \bw_{t,1}$
  \ElsIf{feedback and $t = mN_0$}
    \State $\bw_{t,1}, \bw_{t,2} \gets \lambda_t\bw_{t,1} + (1 - \lambda_t)\bw_{t,2}$
  \EndIf
  \State $\lambda_{t+1} \gets \sgm(a_{t+1})$,\;
         $\hat\bw_t \gets \lambda_{t+1}\bw_{t,1} + (1 - \lambda_{t+1})\bw_{t,2}$
         \Comment{output}
\EndFor
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\noindent The output $\hat\bw_t$ is formed after the sharing, with the new weight $\lambda_{t+1}$, as $\hat\bw_t$ of
Algorithm~2 is formed with $\mu_{t,j}$. It is the vector that predicts $y_{t+1}$. The algorithm takes the operations
of two fKFs plus $O(M)$ for the combination, and $O(M)$ more on the steps where a scheme shares.
